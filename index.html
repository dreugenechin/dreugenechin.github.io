Research Areas

Access to Care

I examine access to care for heterogeneous populations, building and testing conceptual models that explain treatment initiation and engagement outcomes. I use quantitative and mixed-methods research designs.

Measurement Development

I help researchers build and test psychological measures based on reflective, formative, or composite measurement models. I use structural equation modeling to answer research questions.

Well-Being & Flourishing

I examine psychological and religious/spiritual factors that contribute to well-being, burnout, and/or flourishing. I use general linear models and path analytic frameworks.
